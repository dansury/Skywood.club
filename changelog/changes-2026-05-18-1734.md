# 2026-05-18 — Product card gallery arrows and video controls

Replaced the desktop hover-position scrolling of product card galleries
with explicit navigation arrows, and gave card videos native playback
controls.

## Changed

- `js/store.js` — product card media:
  - removed `mousemove`/`mouseleave` gallery navigation (cursor-position
    scrubbing and reset-to-first-frame on leave); videos are no longer
    interrupted by hover, so they play through fully.
  - added semi-transparent semicircular prev/next arrows over the media,
    rendered when a card has more than one frame; navigation wraps around.
  - card `<video>` now carries `controls` (no longer `aria-hidden`),
    enabling scrubbing forward/backward, pause and playback-speed change.
- `css/styles.css` — added `.gallery-arrow` styles (semicircular pills
  flush with the left/right media edges); card video no longer scales on
  card hover so its native controls stay crisp; product dots hide while
  the video frame is active to avoid overlapping the native control bar.
- `spec/frontend.md` — updated the catalog gallery description.
