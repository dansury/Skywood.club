# 2026-05-18 — Restore lazy card video loading after window.load

Reverted the eager video preload. Card videos again render with
`preload="none"` + `data-src`, but `loadProductVideos()` runs as soon as
`window.load` fires — videos start fetching right after the site finishes
loading, so they are ready by the time the user navigates the gallery,
without competing with critical resources during the initial page load.

## Changed

- `js/store.js` — restored `loadProductVideos()` and its `window.load`
  scheduling, the `data-src`/`preload="none"` card video markup, and the
  `data-src` fallback in `setImage()`.
- `spec/frontend.md` — updated the card video loading description.
