# 2026-05-18 — Preload product card videos eagerly

Follow-up to the card gallery arrows change. Card videos were loaded
lazily (`preload="none"` + `data-src`, source set after `window.load`),
so they could still be unbuffered when the user reached the video frame.

## Changed

- `js/store.js` — card `<video>` now renders with a direct `src` and
  `preload="auto"`, so the browser starts fetching every product video as
  soon as the catalog is rendered. Removed `loadProductVideos()` and its
  `window.load` scheduling, and the `data-src` fallback in `setImage()`.
- `spec/frontend.md` — updated the card video loading description.
